Top Food Hotspots in Sydney

Harris Park

“Little India” of Sydney — great for chaat, dosas, and sweets.

Newtown

Hipster central with food trucks, vegan eats, and bold global flavors.

Chinatown (Haymarket)

Noodle carts, bao buns, and bubble tea in Sydney's busiest Asian market.

Parramatta

Multicultural food hub with Middle Eastern grills and Indian snacks.

Surry Hills

Food festivals and gourmet street eats every weekend.

Bankstown

Home to That Lebanese Guy and other legendary grill vendors.

Chatswood

Try Japanese ramen and dumpling stalls near the station.

Sydney CBD

Workday food trucks, night markets, and gourmet kebabs.

Burwood

Asian street food and the famous Gong Cha bubble tea van.

Blacktown

Desi food vans, samosa stalls, and sugarcane juice during fairs.
